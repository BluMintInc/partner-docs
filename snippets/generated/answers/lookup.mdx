| Situation | Status | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You know the player | `200` | At registration, the player can register. Before a create, the create identifies the player by the id you answered. |
| You don't know the player | `200` | At registration, the player is asked to connect their account in your game, then check again. Before a create, BluMint asks the player to connect their account and retries under the same key every 60 seconds. A player still unlinked when the tournament's hold length runs out is disqualified. |
| You are down or overloaded | `502`, `503`, `504`, `408`, `429`, or no answer | At registration, the player is told you aren't responding and to try again in a minute. Before a create, BluMint tries the create again under the same key, as it does when your create URL is down, then the host takes the match and starts it manually. |
| BluMint's request breaks the rules in this guide | `400` | At registration, the player can't register. Before a create, the host takes the match and starts it manually. |
| Your server failed on this request | `500` | At registration, the player is told you aren't responding and to try again in a minute. Before a create, the host takes the match and starts it manually. |
